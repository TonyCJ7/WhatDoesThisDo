## Documentation (WhatDoesThisDo)

Functions, methods, classes, types and constants are documented in markdown pages under
`docs/whatdoesthisdo/references/`, shown on hover by the WhatDoesThisDo extension. Before changing one of them,
read its entry. After creating, changing, renaming, moving or deleting one, write, update or remove its entry in
the same change, following the `whatdoesthisdo` skill.
